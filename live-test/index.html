How Tarpit decides

It asks 3 simple questions about every try at the door:

  • Did it follow the normal steps? Or did it skip ahead and go straight for secrets?
  • Did it act like a real person? People are a bit uneven. Unauthorized AI bots are too fast, or too perfect.
  • Did other unauthorized AI bots attack at the same time? Unauthorized AI bots often work in a group.

Lots of “no” answers = high danger score. A score of 70 or more sounds the alarm.

What Tarpit is for

Tarpit sits at a login door, spots unauthorized AI bots trying to get in, and warns the person in charge. The person decides what happens next — Tarpit does not act alone.

Real break-ins like that — unauthorized AI bots getting in, as happened at Hugging Face in July 2026 — are what Tarpit is built to stop.
